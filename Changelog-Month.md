- Dependencies

### Casino Knight

- Description: Lets go gambling! Adds a casino with slot machines into Dirtmouth to spend geo on. Win big!

### ExtraSpells

- Description: A mod that adds a handful of new spells (and 1-ish nail art). Cycle by selecting the spell in the inventory.

- Tags
