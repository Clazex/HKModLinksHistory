## New mods

### Back Dash

- Description: Brings back the cut beta back dash: dash backwards without turning around, also as a shadow dash.
- Tags
  + Gameplay
  + LLM-Assisted

### Champion&#x27;s Shell

- Description: Adds Champion&#x27;s Shell, a 1-notch charm found in Dirtmouth that shows enemy health bars. Upgrade it at the Nailsmith with geo and relics to deal bonus nail damage to nearly dead enemies.
- Dependencies
  + SFCore
- Tags
  + Charm
  + LLM-Assisted

### CreatureSpawner

- Description: Spawn any creature or boss anywhere with a hotkey (Ctrl by default; changeable in Mods &gt; Creature Spawner).
- Tags
  + Boss
  + LLM-Assisted
  + Utility

### Gaslight

- Description: FPS and mod load profiler. Shows what eats your FPS: in-game graph, per-mod and per-method timings, lag spikes with the culprit, HTML report. Observer only; interface in English and Russian.
- Tags
  + Utility

### GeoShare

- Description: Allows players to transfer Geo in HKMP lobbies using chat commands.
- Dependencies
  + HKMP
- Tags
