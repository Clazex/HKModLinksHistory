## New mods

### DistantGreens Charms

- Description: Adds some custom Charms into the Hollow Knight.
- Dependencies
  + ItemChanger
  + SFCore
- Integrations
  + DebugMod
  + Randomizer 4
- Tags

### Mythril Soul

- Description: Adds a new gamemode to Hollow Knight with permadeath expanded to dreams and pausing disabled at or below two masks (harder steel soul).
- Dependencies
  + Satchel
- Tags
  + Expansion
  + Gameplay
